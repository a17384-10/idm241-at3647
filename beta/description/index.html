Object: Tubi Poster Interaction

TRIGGERS:

  • hovering over any poster
  • hover off the poster
  • hovering over the play button
    • click on the play button
    • hover off the play button
  • hover on the add to my list button
    • toggle on add to my list
    • toggle off add to my list
    • hover off the add to my list button

RULES:

hovering over the poster

  • the cursor becomes a pointer finger
  • the poster picture slightly enlarges, but the frame stays the same size
  • the poster picture becomes slightly darker
  • the poster picture becomes slightly blurry
  • there is a yellow glow behind the poster
  • a yellow play button fades onto in the middle of the poster and the opacity is set to 100
  • a long round button fades onto the bottom of the poster with the text "Add to My List" at the bottom of the poster
  • the yellow play button has a pulsating animation of a shadow emerging from behind the button of which lowers the opacity until a new one appears again

hovering off of the poster

  • the poster picture shrinks back to its original size
  • the poster picture fades back to its original brightness
  • the play button fades out and the opacity is set to zero
  • the text "Add to My List" disappears
  • the poster picture goes back to its original size and brightness
  • the poster picture is no longer blurry
  • the cursor goes back to the default arrow

hovering on the play button

  • the play button enlarges slightly
  • the cursor remains a pointer finger
  • the play button stops pulsating and instead creates a subtle glow effect behind the button
  • the cursor remains a pointer finger

clicking on the play button

  • the play button becomes a little smaller until the mouse releases it
  • the play button goes back to its original size when hovered over
  • no other changes to the play button in terms of appearance

hovering off of the play button

  • the play button goes back to its original size when defaulted
  • the glow disappears and is replaced by the same sort of pulsating animation as before

hovering on the add to my list button

  • the text fades from white to yellow
  • the cursor remains a pointer finger
  • the border fades from a dark gray to yellow
  • the button has a faint yellow glow around it that shows from the sheer background
  • the button has a slight upward movement

hovering off the add to my list button

  • the button goes back to its original position
  • the text fades back from yellow to white
  • the button has a slight downward movement
  • the button loses the faint yellow glow around it
  • the border fades back from yellow to dark gray

toggle on the add to my list button

  • the button fades from a sheer gray to a solid yellow
  • the text fades from yellow to black
  • the button has a slight downward movement before going back to place
  • the text changes from "Add to My List" to "✓ Added to List"
  • there is a sparkle animation happening behind the button that lasts about one second
  • the button has a brighter yellow glow behind it

toggle off the add to my list button

  • the button fades from a solid yellow back to a sheer gray
  • the text fades from black back to yellow
  • the text changes from "✓ Added to List" back to "Add to My List"
